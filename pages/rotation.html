How it works

01

Description

Torus . a 3D object with 4 sides rotating radomly with different locatons around a circle and with various speeds and direction along all 3 axises of rotation

02

Logic used

Points along the the path of circles of different radius was found and origins were translated to that point , the objects are being displayed on those locations and rotated along different axis

03

Functions used

translate( ) - to move the origin to a particular position in the canvas

pointOnCircle( ) - to calculate the coordiantes a round a given circle
