Under the hood

How it works

01

Description

Each instrument is analysed as it plays. Its frequencies become a ring of bars that reach both outward and inward, its waveform becomes a wobbling circle in the middle, and the recent past drifts outward as fading ripples. The middle ring swells with the bass.

02

Logic used

p5.FFT splits the audio into 1024 frequency values. They are grouped into 72 bands on a logarithmic scale, mirrored left and right, and drawn as bars that grow outward with the level and inward on louder hits, with slowly falling peak markers. The waveform is bent into a ring, and a copy is saved every few frames so older rings move outward and fade.

03

Functions used

analyze( )

Amplitude values along the frequency domain, from low to high.

waveform( )

Amplitude values along the time domain.

getEnergy( )

Average energy of a frequency range, here the bass.
