Under the hood

How it works

01

Description

Choose a bundled music track and explore its changing spectrum, waveform and circular audio visualisation.

02

Logic used

p5.FFT analyses the audio in both the frequency and time domains. Frequency values drive the spectrum bars, while waveform samples are drawn as curves.

03

Functions used

waveform( ) - computes amplitude values along the time domain.

analyze( ) - amplitude values along the frequency domain.Frequencies from the low to the high
